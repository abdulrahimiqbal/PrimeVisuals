## Why no breakthrough survives

1. **Effectivity is insufficient.**  The controls have positive integral
   points and closed orbits in every degree.  More strongly, their zeta-series
   coefficients satisfy the exact genus-one Riemann–Roch profile
   `A_n=h(q^n-1)/(q-1)` with `h=(r-1)(s-1)>0`.
2. **Restriction compatibility is insufficient.**  The controls commute with
   every base extension via `N_(m,n)=N_(mn)` and retain positive orbit counts.
3. **Purity is inadmissible as an axiom.**  It is the requested conclusion.
4. **An arbitrary compatible positive form is circular.**  Its existence is
   equivalent to diagonalizability with all roots on the square-root circle.
5. **The non-circular survivor is classical.**  Constructing the form from an
   ample polarization and proving `pi^dagger pi=q` is the standard
   Weil/Rosati proof for abelian varieties, followed by the standard Jacobian
   comparison for curves.
6. **There is no independent new payoff.**  Once the known finite-field
   square-root theorem is removed, this campaign leaves an axiom-independence
   obstruction, not a new theorem about curves, primes, zeta functions, or
   arithmetic families.

Pass gates P1–P4 are met only by the known Rosati package.  P5 fails.  Kill
gates K1–K5 all fire in the appropriate lanes.  The target is therefore not
parked: its mathematical content is settled well enough to rule it out as one
of the five field-breakthrough candidates.
