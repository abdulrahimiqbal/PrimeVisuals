# Frobenius-tuples kill campaign

This directory contains the complete campaign for Atlas frontier 3.

- `PREREGISTRATION.md` — frozen theorem object, family, holdouts, controls, and
  promotion gates.
- `run-audit.mjs` — deterministic integer/control/finite-local audit.
- `results.json` — full structured output, including all frozen control runs.
- `cell-data.csv` — real-prime cumulative and disjoint-block cell values.
- `EVIDENCE.md` — compact numerical evidence.
- `NOVELTY_AUDIT.md` — reduction to prime pairs in progressions and nearest
  primary literature.
- `VERDICT.md` — final kill decision and restart condition.

The audited statistic is a centered product of quadratic Frobenius signs on
fixed-gap prime pairs. It fails the numerical gates and reduces to a zero-sum
Hardy--Littlewood-in-progressions contrast.
