# Atlas five-frontier kill campaign

Date: 2026-07-12

This directory records preregistration, evidence, source audit, controls, and a
verdict for each of the five highest-ranked theorem-search routes in the Prime
Theory Atlas.

The common field-level rule was strict: a route had to produce a precise new
statement, survive known-math/disguise and hostile-control audits, clear its
proof obligation, and change a theorem-level consequence.  A useful identity,
instrument, or negative theorem could survive as infrastructure without being
called a breakthrough.

## Field-level verdict

**Zero of five routes survives as a field-level breakthrough theorem or as a
currently justified privileged breakthrough lead.**

Three pieces of durable mathematical infrastructure remain: the P3
same-coordinate resolvent formula, the finite-field Rosati hostile control,
and the exact Maynard certification engine.  They narrow future search; none
supplies a new prime theorem.

The cleanest consequence-changing reopen named by this audit is an exact
rational `M_53>4` certificate, which the Polymath8b implication chain would
turn into an Elliott--Halberstam two-gap improvement from 270 to 264.  It is a
valid target, not evidence that the present basis is close to proving it.

Each subdirectory contains its own reopen conditions.  Do not revive a killed
route by changing a plot, modulus, seed, window, or basis depth while leaving
the failed mechanism and consequence gates unchanged.
