# Preregistrations

## Common promotion gate

A route promotes only with a standalone theorem, complete proof, checked
external dependencies, an explicit RH-relevant consequence, and hostile
novelty/circularity review. Numerical evidence may choose which exact statement
to attack; it cannot close an infinite claim.

## Common kill gate

Kill or park a route if its minimal lemma is RH or a catalogued equivalent in
new notation, if the proposed object is an invertible transform of a known
RH-sensitive object, if a standard resonator/control saturates the desired
bound, if the downstream consequence does not follow, or if the only proposed
progress is a larger finite basis/range.

## Discovery/validation separation

Any computation used to select a candidate must freeze its statement and
parameters before a disjoint holdout. Exact witnesses are reevaluated in the
original functional. Floating generalized eigenvalues are diagnostic only.

## Twelve rounds

The exact targets, minimal lemmas, and falsifiers for rounds 1--12 are the
corresponding frozen rows of `APPROACH_REGISTRY.md`. Each round must end in
`PROMOTED`, `PARKED`, `BLOCKED`, or `KILLED`, with a written reason.
