## 1. Rigorous lemmas actually proved

1. **Analytic zero-amplification countermodel.** An explicit real, even,
   order-one canonical product has the completed-zeta functional symmetry,
   Riemann--von Mangoldt leading zero count, and exactly one off-line quartet.
   Therefore functional equation, conjugation, finite order, and leading zero
   density alone do not amplify one bad zero. A separate Rouché/packing lemma
   shows exactly how positive-density recurrence would create linearly many
   zeros.
2. **Hardy-space boundary-modulus no-go.** Multiplication by a finite Blaschke
   product inserts arbitrary finite zeros without changing any weighted
   boundary `L^2` modulus. Boundary-modulus mollifier coercivity alone cannot
   exclude an isolated zero.
3. **Nyman--Beurling compactness calibration.** Nested orthogonal projections
   already converge strongly to the projection onto the closed union. An
   explicit `ell^2` example has `1/(N log^2 N)` innovations and a compact
   residual orbit while retaining a positive limiting distance. Compactness is
   not a closure mechanism.
4. **Finite-place Sonin projection theorem.** For every finite prime set, the
   Euler multiplier is boundedly invertible, the semilocal Sonin projection has
   an exact range-projection formula, and admissible compressed traces are
   Hilbert--Schmidt squares. The final projection is independent of the order
   of adjoining primes. Kronecker density also gives the exact raw-multiplier
   condition number, which diverges along growing prime sets; this obstructs
   scalar-normalized raw uniform metric equivalence, not arbitrary global
   closure.
5. **Actual-coefficient shifted Möbius decomposition.** A prime-divisor
   identity writes binary Möbius correlation as specific prime-supported
   shifted bilinear boxes. Cauchy/dispersion reduces a balanced box to averaged
   growing-slope affine Chowla correlations; separate Type-I and shifted-prime
   endpoints remain.
6. **Exact Maynard threshold certificate lemma.** For rational Gram matrices,
   `lambda_max(A,I)>tau` is equivalent to positive inertia of `A-tau I` and to
   an exact rational witness. A dimension-14 rational `LDL^T` proof of concept
   correctly brackets the known degree-5 optimum.

These are rigorous campaign/repository lemmas. The literature audit found
them to be elementary, standard, or direct corollaries of published
frameworks; none is asserted to be a new field-level theorem.

## 6. Why the missing lemma is not RH in disguise

It is a finite rational inequality in an explicit sieve variational problem.
Its consequence is a bounded-gap improvement, not RH, a zero-free statement,
or an RH-equivalent positivity criterion. The obstruction is numerical and
variational conditioning, followed by exact certification—not hidden zeta-zero
cancellation.

## 7. Conditions required to reopen parked routes

- **de Branges/Suzuki:** a coefficient-side proof of all-time Fredholm
  nonvanishing and terminal kernel decay, with no inserted zeros, innerness, or
  Weil positivity assumption.
- **Arithmetic Hodge effectivity:** a finite-support geometric comparison
  functor into the cyclic/Sonin carrier, a same-coordinate remainder identity,
  invariant domains, and a sign theorem rejecting the RH-false controls.
- **Shifted Möbius:** both a uniform power-saving averaged growing-slope affine
  correlation theorem on the actual balanced boxes and power-saving Type-I/
  shifted-prime endpoint estimates.
- **Maynard 49:** the stable `k=50` control, then a rational `k=49` vector and
  independent exact `N-4D>0` certificate. The low-degree inertia test alone is
  insufficient.
- **Global Weil decomposition:** independently defined, restriction-compatible
  atoms with uniform closure; neither a larger local dictionary nor an
  arbitrary fitted PSD block qualifies.
- **Family density:** an explicit individualizing amplifier whose full
  diagonal/off-diagonal cost preserves a strict zeta saving.
