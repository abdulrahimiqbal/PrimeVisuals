# Proof dependencies

No qualifying theorem has yet been promoted. External results and their exact
roles are appended here for every rigorous lemma or terminal theorem actually
proved during the campaign.

## Round 1

- Weierstrass/canonical-product convergence: used to construct `H` from
  `sum t_n^(-2)<infinity`.
- Rouché's theorem: turns each sufficiently accurate translated boundary
  recurrence into a zero in the translated disk.
- A sublinear zeta zero-density estimate for each fixed `sigma>1/2`: contradicts
  positive-linear recurrence amplification.
- Bagchi's recurrence theorem: identifies the needed positive-density strong
  self-recurrence as RH-equivalent.

The explicit countermodel and separation count otherwise use only elementary
entire-function and packing arguments.

## Round 3

- Hermite--Biehler strict inequality: proves that an exact positive companion
  for `Xi` forces all its zeros real.
- Herglotz representation and de Branges inverse theorem: show that spectrum
  alone omits norming weights and boundary normalization.
- Canonical-system Lagrange identity: converts a positive Hamiltonian plus a
  vanishing terminal boundary term into positive de Branges kernels.
- Suzuki's Fredholm construction and equivalence theorem: supplies the local
  coefficient-side Hamiltonian and identifies the global endpoint package with
  RH.

## Round 4

- Orthogonal projection/Pythagoras theorem: proves automatic strong convergence
  of nested projections and the exact innovation identity.
- Báez--Duarte criterion: identifies the zero limiting distance with RH.
- Compact operators send bounded weakly convergent sequences to norm-convergent
  images: rules out compact coercivity on an infinite-dimensional residual
  space.
- Burnol lower bound: excludes overly strong log-free quantitative rates in the
  Nyman--Beurling distance.

## Round 6

- Connes--Consani--Moscovici finite-place Mellin identification: supplies
  `M_F=D_FM` for finite semilocal place sets.
- Bounded-operator range-projection identity: proves the explicit `P_F` formula
  and path independence.
- Hilbert--Schmidt ideal property and trace identity: proves finite positive
  compressed traces.
- Kronecker density plus rational independence of prime logarithms: identifies
  the essential extrema of each finite Euler multiplier exactly.
- Euler's divergence of `sum_p 1/p` plus elementary logarithmic inequalities:
  proves divergence of the exact raw-multiplier condition number.
- Closed skew-adjoint spectral theorem: places the spectrum of
  `Theta-1/2` on the imaginary axis once the full positive carrier/domain
  hypotheses are independently established.

## Round 8

- Weak-* Furstenberg correspondence: turns coordinate cylinder integrals into
  arithmetic correlations.
- Walsh inversion on finite sign cubes: proves equivalence between cylinder
  discrepancy and the family of finite correlations.
- Elementary stationary Markov-chain calculation and Bernoulli orthogonality:
  provide the mixing and no-Koopman-gap controls.
- Block concatenation/generic-point construction: separates qualitative
  Bernoulli genericity from any prescribed empirical convergence rate.

## Round 7

- Elementary squarefree case split: proves the exact prime-divisor identity.
- Cauchy--Schwarz and the affine gcd identity: produce the precise dispersion
  off-diagonal and local factors.
- Sharp `p=1` Khintchine inequality: supplies the arbitrary cut-norm lower
  bound through random signs.
- Published logarithmic/averaged Chowla theorems: calibrate the gap; none is
  used to claim the missing power-saving estimate.

## Round 9

- Sylvester inertia under congruence and rational symmetric `LDL^T`: decide
  whether the threshold form has a positive direction.
- Density of rational vectors plus openness of strict positivity: turns a real
  positive direction into an exact rational witness.
- Polymath8b enlarged-support theorem: supplies the `k=50` calibration and the
  downstream consequence that a future `k=49` certificate would activate.
