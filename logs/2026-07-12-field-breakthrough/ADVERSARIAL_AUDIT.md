# Adversarial audit

No candidate is presumed correct. This file records attempts to find
counterexamples, circularity, hidden conditional assumptions, domain failures,
nonuniform constants, invalid limit passages, finite-to-infinite gaps, and
prior-art containment.

## Round 1

Functional equation, conjugation, finite order, and the leading zero-count
asymptotic were all built into an explicit countermodel with only one off-line
quartet. Deuring--Heilbronn concerns exceptional real zeros in `L`-function
families and does not replicate a nonreal zeta zero. Turán/zero-detecting
methods give density upper bounds, not vertical replication. Positive-density
self-recurrence on the selected disk would work by Rouché; global Bagchi
recurrence is RH-equivalent, while the disk property quantified over all zeros
in a fixed half-plane is equivalent by implication and vacuity to that
fixed-half-plane zero-free conclusion. The hostile audit also corrected
`t_n~2 pi n/log n` and verified the canonical-product order and packing count.

## Round 2

The candidate's zero-exclusion implication failed before coefficient or
conditioning analysis. Boundary `L^2` coercivity ignores inner factors; point
evaluation coercivity would simply restate zero-freeness. No RH consequence is
claimed.

## Round 3

Three insertion paths were separated. `Xi` is self-conjugate and cannot itself
be Hermite--Biehler; an exact positive companion proves real-rootedness; and a
real spectrum without norming weights underdetermines the Hamiltonian. The
remaining Suzuki construction is coefficient-side and local, but global
Fredholm invertibility plus the endpoint condition is precisely the unproved
RH-equivalent package.

## Round 4

An explicit `ell^2` construction matches scale-stable `1/(N log^2 N)`
innovations while retaining a positive limiting distance. Generic compact
observability was also ruled out on infinite-dimensional residual spaces by an
orthonormal weakly-null sequence. Finite Schur-flow regularity therefore cannot
support the closure claim.

## Round 5

The audit applied the repository's exact `N=8` separator to the restrictive
end of the proposed atom class and the spectral theorem to the unrestricted
end. The former fails; the latter merely restates finite PSD. No
finite-to-infinite closure or independent arithmetic atom was supplied.

## Round 6

The finite-set projection formula was checked by writing it as the range
projection of an explicit isometry. Compatibility follows from uniqueness of
orthogonal projection, not a fitted numerical pattern. The hostile audit added
the fixed-cutoff and Hilbert--Schmidt domain hypotheses and restricted path
independence to the final projection. Kronecker density proves the raw
multiplier condition number really diverges, but the example `M=H` shows this
does not obstruct convergence of the projections or traces. Only scalar-
normalized raw-multiplier uniform metric equivalence is ruled out. Positivity
of the trace is not confused with invariance or skew-adjoint closure of the
spectral generator.

## Round 12

The proposed broad obstruction collapsed to a negative direct-sum extension.
The adversarial converse shows its limitation: positivity on a nested dense
union does extend to a bounded self-adjoint operator. Hence the lemma cannot
materially redirect operator approaches and is not promoted as new.

## Round 10

An extremal allocation placed the entire permitted family zero count on the
distinguished member. This saturates the only bound obtainable from positivity
of the family counts. The claimed specialization therefore fails unless an
amplifier with separately audited cost is supplied.

## Round 11

The statistic was expanded before computation. Its cubic term is exactly a
weighted triple-correlation sum, with repeated-shift diagonals separated
algebraically. This prevents a new coordinate from being mistaken for a new
cancellation mechanism. No empirical scan was run.

## Round 8

The proposed spectral gap was tested on the ideal Bernoulli model and failed
via explicit approximate invariants. Exponential mixing was tested on a
two-state Markov chain and did not kill adjacent correlation. Finally, a
Bernoulli-generic block construction separated limiting-system identification
from quantitative convergence of the deterministic Liouville point.

## Round 9

The threshold-inertia criterion was checked on exact rational matrices at two
sides of a known low-degree optimum. It avoids the killed whitening/cutoff
logic, but coefficient swell appears by dimension 14 and no degree-27 interval
pivot or rational witness exists. Published `k=50` reproduction remains the
mandatory control; no consequence is claimed from the low-degree check.

## Round 7

The unshifted saturation control was separated from the genuinely shifted
matrix. A Khintchine cut-norm lower bound limits but does not refute a smaller
power saving. Cauchy/dispersion was expanded completely; its off-diagonal is
not a simpler bilinear object but a growing-slope two-affine-form Möbius
correlation. The hostile audit repaired the dropped `p not|r` condition before
Cauchy, exposed the quarter-exponent loss, and separated a trivial endpoint
upper bound from actual size. Balanced Type II alone cannot support the claimed
downstream theorem, and the complete suite is not known weaker than Chowla.
