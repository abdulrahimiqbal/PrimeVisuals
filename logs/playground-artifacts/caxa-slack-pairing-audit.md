## Factor check

This statistic uses row-level CA/XA prime-step margins, not `pi`, `psi`, zeta, zeros, or gap-width buckets. The break is internal: `rho` is built directly from the same local margin formula that defines recovery. It is therefore a CA/XA bookkeeping invariant unless it can be proven or compared against same-resolution fake path rows.
