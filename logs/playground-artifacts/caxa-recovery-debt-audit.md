## Factor check

This is not a linear transform of `pi`, `psi`, or a named gap-width residual. It is built from CA/XA transition log margins. But it is also not an independent critical line yet: `B(Y)` is exactly a finite prefix of the existing CA/XA no-base recovery conjecture, and the fake-shape null is heterogeneous, including seeds with zero no-base events and seeds with long unrecovered tails.
