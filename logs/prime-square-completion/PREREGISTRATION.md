# Preregistration: prime-square completion kill test

Frozen: 2026-07-12, before cone feasibility results were inspected.

## Representation

For an integer horizon `N >= 8`, use the exact log partition

`0, log 2, log 3, ..., log N`

of `L^2(0, log N)`.  Its cell basis is
`chi_i = 1_(log i, log(i+1)]`, for `i=1,...,N-1`.  Translation by
`log m` is represented without interpolation; in the anchored basis it obeys
`T_log(m) T_log(n) = T_log(mn)` exactly, including the finite-horizon
truncation.

The target `M_N` is the mixed-difference matrix of Suzuki's complete zeta
screw kernel on this partition.  The metric-strain normalization must first
reproduce the prime portion of `M_N` with the correct minus sign.

## Frozen cones

Put `Delta_p = I - T_log(p)` and
`Box_(p,q) = Delta_p Delta_q`.  Only closed divisor squares `p*q < N` enter.

The strict width-one LP cone is generated by

- the cell norm `R(0,0)`;
- `Gram(Delta_p)` for every prime `p < N`.

The strict width-two LP cone adds

- `Gram(Box_(p,q))` for every `p < q` with `p*q < N`.

All coefficients must be nonnegative.  There is no unspecified PSD remainder,
target-dependent basis, eigenvector-selected atom, future-prime atom, or fitted
Cholesky factor.

If the strict LP fails, the only preregistered enlargement is the width-two SDP
cone.  For each closed pair `(p,q)`, let

`F_(p,q) = (Delta_p, Delta_q, Delta_p Delta_q)`.

It permits a positive `3 x 3` Gram block on these three operators, plus the
same nonnegative cell-norm and single-edge atoms.  This remains local to two
prime directions.  No other enlargement counts as survival of this
conjecture.

## Cells

- discovery: `N = 8, 12, 18, 26`
- unseen holdout: `N = 10, 15, 22, 30`

## Decisions

- **Normalization kill:** strain or sign error above `1e-10`.
- **Strict-LP survivor:** relative Frobenius residual below `1e-8` in every
  discovery and holdout cell with nonnegative coefficients.
- **Width-two survivor:** the SDP enlargement is feasible to relative residual
  below `1e-8` in every cell.
- **Numerical death candidate:** a dual separator has atom margins at least
  `1e-7` and separates the target by at least `1e-7` relative Frobenius scale.
- **Hard death:** the separator is rationalized or enclosed by rigorous
  intervals, proving nonmembership in the frozen cone.

Finite survival promotes only to `RESEARCH LEAD`.  Field-level status requires
one symbolic coefficient rule for all horizons/scales, the actual
archimedean/boundary terms, compatible restriction maps, uniform coefficient
bounds, failure on a matched RH-false model, and the analytic passage to the
full Weil test space.
