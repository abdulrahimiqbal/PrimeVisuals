# Prime-square completion verdict

Status: **HARD-KILLED AS FROZEN / METRIC-STRAIN IDENTITY SURVIVES /
DIVISOR-CUBE PROGRAM OPEN**.

## What was tested

The preregistered target was the log-cell compression of Suzuki's complete
zeta screw kernel.  The frozen width-two cone contained

- one nonnegative cell-norm atom;
- all nonnegative prime-edge Grams `Gram(Delta_p)`;
- for every closed square `p*q < N`, one positive `3 x 3` Gram block on
  `(Delta_p, Delta_q, Delta_p Delta_q)`.

There was no arbitrary PSD remainder, target-adapted basis, fitted Cholesky
factor, or post-hoc boundary term.

The normalization gate passed: after Brownian/Volterra congruence, the prime
screw block is the negative of the pulled-back Euler-metric strain, with
maximum errors below `3.6e-15` in the exported cells.

## Result

All discovery and holdout cells failed by macroscopic margins.  Relative
projection residuals for the width-two SDP cone ranged from `0.7738` at `N=8`
to `0.8429` at `N=30`.  The targets themselves are positive definite, so this
is not a trivial failure caused by asking a PSD cone to contain an indefinite
matrix.

The raw optimizer residual has active dual margins near zero.  It therefore
does not satisfy the preregistered `+1e-7` numerical-separator gate; the solver
report correctly labels that intermediate calculation inconclusive.  A
separate strengthened witness supplies the hard result.

## Exact separator at N=8

A symmetric rational `7 x 7` matrix `Y`, with denominator `1,000,000`, was
constructed from the numerical dual and shifted into the strict dual
interior.  Every inequality was then recomputed independently from the
defining formulas using 256-bit Arb ball arithmetic.

- boundary pairing: `+0.10397200448422888856...`;
- prime-edge pairings: between `+0.1124` and `+0.2243`;
- the `(2,3)` pair-block adjoint has positive leading principal minors
  `0.17737...`, `0.025855...`, and `0.0034818...`;
- target pairing: `-0.19537686545848761491...`.

Thus `<Y,C> >= 0` for every element `C` of the frozen cone, while
`<Y,M_8> < 0`.  This proves `M_8` is outside the cone.  Since the proposed rule
was required to hold at every horizon and included `N=8`, one exact
counterexample kills it.

The target enclosure includes explicit positive-tail control of the Lerch
series and rigorous balls for the archimedean constants.  The cone geometry
uses exact integer endpoint comparisons; every nonzero overlap is a logarithm
of a rational.  The certificate is rigorous but has not yet been replayed in
Lean; a Lean port would need formal bounds for the same constants and series.

## Independent audit

An independent implementation audit found no sign, basis, or SDP-factor bug:

- mixed differences are exactly the cell-to-anchored congruence;
- continuous and anchored edge/plaquette Grams agree within `2.7e-15`;
- all nine ordered SDP cross-terms are present, so no factor of two is lost;
- the prime target equals negative strain within `3.6e-15`;
- the Arb pairings agree with the floating export to ordinary rounding error.

## What died, and what did not

The result kills the flat three-generator prime-square grammar.  It does not
kill the Euler-metric strain identity, all two-prime-local operator algebras,
higher or position-dependent Hodge weights, open boundary cells, or the broad
idea of lifting prime powers to the divisor-exponent lattice.

Post-hoc diagnostics show that the failure is not repaired by the most obvious
coefficient enrichments.  Adding the identity channel to every local block
still leaves `75%--84%` residual.  Coupling all existing edge and plaquette
operators in one global PSD block leaves `72%--81%`.  Adding repeated-axis
difference powers through degree three also leaves `75%--84%`.  These were
diagnostics, not preregistered survivors.

The lesson is structural: the complete Weil form is positive, but its
positivity is not generated by a flat bounded dictionary of causal prime
differences.  Merely increasing polynomial degree, prime width, or coefficient
freedom is not a breakthrough program.

## Promotion rule retained

A finite survivor would only have been a research lead.  Field-level status
would require one scale-independent symbolic decomposition, a derived
archimedean/boundary mechanism, compatible restriction maps, uniform bounds,
failure on matched RH-false controls, and analytic closure to the complete
Weil test space.  Positivity on that full dense core, followed by the closure
argument, would be the RH-level endpoint.
