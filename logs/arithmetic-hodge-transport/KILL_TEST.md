# Arithmetic Hodge--Transport program: hostile kill test

Date: 2026-07-12
Frozen criteria: `KILL_TEST_PREREGISTRATION.md`
Verdict: **SURVIVE, NARROW**

## Executive result

The candidate-neutral direction is not killed and does not meet the park
conditions.  It is worth an atlas, but only as an obstruction-first program
focused on global gluing, independently derived polarization, and analytic
closure.

The test found no universal impossibility theorem, unavoidable circularity, or
collapse of every intermediate statement to RH.  Several carriers are defined
without using a zero list and already support nontrivial pieces of the desired
architecture.  Exact RH-false controls also separate orbit/trace data,
functional-equation duality, and positive polarization.  Thus the missing
polarization has a falsifiable mathematical job.

The direction does **not** earn `SURVIVE, STRONG`.  No existing framework was
found that proves a global, finite-place-sensitive Hodge-index statement with
an unconditional new prime or zero consequence.

## K1 -- no universal impossibility was found

Christopher Deninger proves that no naive Weil cohomology with real
coefficients satisfying the expected axioms can exist even for number rings.
This is a serious design constraint, not a universal kill: the theorem does not
exclude complex or semilinear coefficients, derived/topological categories,
noncommutative spaces, or indefinite algebraic duality followed by a separately
constructed polarization.

Source:

- Christopher Deninger, [There is no "Weil-"cohomology theory with real
  coefficients for arithmetic curves](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.02714).

No primary source located in this audit proves incompatibility of the minimal
orbit/trace, duality, and positive-polarization package across the full
candidate-neutral design space frozen in the preregistration.

## K2 -- independent mathematical carriers exist

The following are not built by fitting zeta zeros:

1. Deninger's rational-Witt construction attaches an infinite-dimensional
   real dynamical system to normal finite-type arithmetic schemes; periodic
   orbit packets are related to closed points, and local points connect to
   Fargues--Fontaine geometry.
2. The Connes--Consani arithmetic/scaling sites supply an adelic/tropical
   carrier, prime periodic orbits, Frobenius correspondences, divisor theory,
   and Riemann--Roch on prime orbits.  A separate Riemann--Roch theorem for
   compactified `Spec Z` is also proved.
3. Their 2025 class-field construction functorially maps finite abelian
   extensions of `Q` to finite covers, with prime-orbit monodromy matching
   Frobenius.
4. Weil--etale complexes encode orders and leading values at integer arguments
   and are compatible with major special-value conjectures in known cases.
5. Prismatic and Cartier--Witt machinery provide genuine local Frobenius,
   slopes, filtrations, and comparison structures at each `p`.

Sources:

- Deninger, [Dynamical systems for arithmetic schemes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06400).
- Connes--Consani, [Geometry of the scaling site](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.03191).
- Connes--Consani, [Riemann--Roch for the ring Z](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.00456).
- Connes--Consani, [Knots, primes and class field theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.06560).
- Flach--Morin, [Weil--etale cohomology and zeta-values of proper regular arithmetic schemes](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.01277).
- Bhatt--Lurie, [Absolute prismatic cohomology](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.06120).

These components do not yet form one theory.  Their independence is enough to
defeat unavoidable circularity, not enough to establish the endpoint.

## K3 -- a non-RH theorem ladder exists

Three precise intermediate classes survive the disguise check.

### I1. Arithmetic orbit--trace realization

For an independently defined arithmetic carrier and a specified nuclear test
algebra, construct `H^i` and a flow generator `Theta`, prove the domains and
trace-class properties, and derive the completed explicit-formula distribution
as a Lefschetz trace formula.  Do not impose positivity.

This is strictly weaker than RH: the exact control family in the next section
has closed-orbit trace data, a determinant rational function, functional
equation, and algebraic duality while RH fails.

### I2. Special-value and archimedean gluing

Construct comparison maps joining Weil--etale/THH-style global complexes,
local `p`-adic realizations, and an archimedean realization for a new class of
arithmetic schemes.  Prove a new special-value or functional-equation formula.

This has independent arithmetic content and is not a zero-location assertion.
Existing Weil--etale and topological Hochschild work provides an actual
foothold rather than only an analogy.

Source:

- Baptiste Morin, [Topological Hochschild homology and zeta-values](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.11549).

### I3. Restricted semi-local polarization

Extend the proved archimedean-place operator positivity to one finite place
`{infinity,p}` for an intrinsic compact-support class, with all boundary and
closure estimates explicit.  A fixed-place theorem is not RH; a compatible
uniform family over all support radii would be the later closure obligation.

The archimedean base case is a real theorem, not a numerical observation.

Source:

- Connes--Consani, [Weil positivity and trace formula, the archimedean place](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.13771).

## K4/S3 -- exact discriminating controls

### Control A: positive-orbit reciprocal systems

For distinct positive integers `u,v`, define

`q=2^(u+v)`, `r=2^u`, `s=2^v`, and

`Z_(u,v)(T)=((1-rT)(1-sT))/((1-T)(1-qT))`.

This family has all of the following:

1. reciprocal numerator and exact functional equation
   `Z_(u,v)(1/(qT))=Z_(u,v)(T)`;
2. trace counts `N_n=q^n+1-r^n-s^n`;
3. nonnegative integer primitive closed-orbit multiplicities `B_n`, hence an
   exact Euler product `prod_m (1-T^m)^(-B_m)`;
4. spectral weights `u/(u+v)` and `v/(u+v)`, reflected about `1/2` but off it;
5. exact weight-one algebraic duality.  With
   `Theta=diag(u/(u+v),v/(u+v))` and `J=[[0,1],[1,0]]`,
   `Theta^T J + J Theta = J`.

The pairing `J` has signature `(1,1)`, not a positive polarization.  The family
therefore predicts a precise failure: a genuine Hodge theory must reject it at
polarization/effectivity, not at orbit counts, Euler product, trace,
reciprocity, or algebraic duality.

Nonnegative orbit multiplicities hold in every degree.  For `n>1`, `B_n` is
the primitive-necklace count on a `q`-letter alphabet minus those on disjoint
`r`- and `s`-letter subalphabets.  Since `q>=r+s`, the latter inject disjointly
into the former.  Degree one has the additional constant orbit.

### Control B: functional equation without Euler geometry

Davenport--Heilbronn Dirichlet series satisfy Riemann-type functional equations
but have zeros off the critical line and lack the required Euler-product
geometry.  This control is predicted to fail at local-factor/orbit
factorization before polarization.

Sources:

- Davenport--Heilbronn, [On the zeros of certain Dirichlet series](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/s1-11.4.307).
- Bombieri--Ghosh, [Around the Davenport--Heilbronn function](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/rm9410).

### Control C: Euler products without the full arithmetic geometry

Beurling generalized prime systems retain literal Euler products and permit a
wide range of analytic and zero behavior.  They are controls for the claim that
an Euler product or prime-orbit system alone supplies duality or polarization.

Source:

- Hilberdink--Lapidus, [Beurling zeta functions, generalised primes, and fractal membranes](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0410270).

## S4 -- workshop leverage

The positive-orbit control is not another finite positivity table.  It is an
exact infinite family proving logical independence among proposed axioms and
providing a mandatory negative control for every atlas candidate.

The existing prime-square program supplies a second rigorous input.  A rational
dual separator proves that the complete compressed Weil target is outside the
frozen cone generated by bounded local prime-edge and prime-square Gram blocks.
That result kills a grammar, not the broad program, and directs the atlas away
from flat bounded local dictionaries toward global, archimedean-aware, and
restriction-compatible structures.

## Why the verdict is narrow

The strongest candidate frameworks remain fragmented.  The square of the
scaling site lacks the required global `H^1`/intersection/effectivity theorem;
Deninger's arithmetic dynamics lacks the full cohomology and determinant
package; Weil--etale theory controls special values rather than the whole zero
spectrum; prismatic theory is local at `p`; and the full finite-place-sensitive
positive polarization remains open.

Very recent 2026 absolute-geometry and Weil-screw preprints add genuine
structure and coordinates, but neither supplies the missing global positive
Hodge theorem:

- Connes--Consani, [On the absolute geometry of Spec Z](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06604).
- Suzuki, [Weil's quadratic form via the screw function](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09096).

## Decision

Build the atlas.  Its unit of progress is not a new scalar invariant or a
larger positive matrix.  It must track:

1. which axioms are independently constructed;
2. which comparison maps commute;
3. exactly where each countermodel fails;
4. which obstruction certificates demand a new global structure; and
5. which intermediate theorem has an independent consequence before RH.
