# Arithmetic Hodge--Transport program: kill-test preregistration

Frozen: 2026-07-12, before the dedicated kill-test audit.

## Question

Is a candidate-neutral Arithmetic Hodge--Transport program worth pursuing as
a research program, or is it presently circular, structurally impossible,
unfalsifiable, or too remote to justify an atlas?

This test does **not** ask whether RH can be proved by one named construction.
It tests the shared program:

1. construct an arithmetic object independently of the zeros;
2. derive prime/archimedean terms from a trace or intersection theory;
3. derive the functional equation from duality;
4. derive critical-line positivity from a polarization or Hodge-index theorem;
5. recover the finite-field theory functorially and extend beyond `K = Q`.

No literal `q -> 1` limit, real-coefficient classical clone, Hilbert space fitted
from a zero list, or positivity axiom equivalent by definition to RH is required
or permitted.  The admissible design space includes derived, topological,
semilinear, tropical, noncommutative, and operator-algebraic realizations.

## Evidence lanes

The audit will examine the following independently:

1. **Logical independence and no-go theorems.**  Does a theorem rule out the
   whole admissible design space, or only naive realizations?  Can trace,
   duality, and polarization be separated logically?
2. **Constructed footholds.**  Is any carrier defined from arithmetic data with
   at least two nontrivial structural components already proved (for example
   orbit/Frobenius correspondence, functoriality, Riemann--Roch, special-value
   cohomology, or an arithmetic Hodge theorem)?
3. **Intermediate theorem ladder.**  Is there at least one precise theorem
   strictly weaker than RH, not merely an equivalent reformulation, whose proof
   would advance the endpoint and have an independent arithmetic consequence?
4. **Falsifiability.**  Can RH-false or geometry-broken controls retain some
   axioms while failing a predicted one, so candidate frameworks can be killed?
5. **Workshop leverage.**  Can PrimeVisuals produce rigorous obstruction,
   compatibility, or finite-model evidence that informs the mathematical
   architecture, rather than only illustrations or larger positivity tables?

Primary sources take precedence.  Fresh 2025--2026 preprints count as evidence
of a construction but not as established closure of a missing theorem.

## Hard kill gates

Kill the whole direction if **any one** of the following is established.

### K1 -- universal impossibility

A valid theorem rules out every admissible category above from simultaneously
supporting the minimal orbit/trace, duality, and positive-polarization package.
A no-go result for only real coefficients, finite-dimensional spaces, or one
specific geometry does not trigger K1.

### K2 -- unavoidable circularity

For every viable carrier, either the cohomology/flow is defined from the zeta
zeros, the pairing is defined from Weil's RH-equivalent form, or the claimed
trace formula inserts the explicit formula as an axiom; and no independently
defined structural component survives after removing that input.

### K3 -- no theorem ladder

Every precise next statement is either already equivalent to RH/GRH, known to
be false, or requires the full missing theory before it can even be stated.
There is no non-RH intermediate theorem with independent content.

### K4 -- observational equivalence

All admissible axioms before full positivity are also satisfied by explicit
RH-false controls, and the only possible discriminator is the assertion of RH
itself.  In that case the program has no falsifiable mechanism.

## Park gates

Park rather than kill if no hard kill fires but both conditions hold:

1. no independently constructed carrier currently has two proved structural
   components relevant to the endpoint; and
2. the first non-circular intermediate theorem has no foothold in existing
   mathematics and no informative finite or formal audit available here.

## Survival gates

The program survives only if all of the following are documented.

### S1 -- independent foothold

At least one carrier is defined from arithmetic/geometric data without using a
zero list and has at least two relevant proved components.  Different
components may also exist in different frameworks; the atlas must record the
missing comparison/gluing problem rather than pretending they are already one
theory.

### S2 -- nontrivial theorem ladder

At least two precise intermediate theorem classes strictly weaker than RH are
identified.  At least one must yield an independent consequence such as a new
special-value, trace, zero-density, zero-free, Chebotarev, or prime-cancellation
statement.

### S3 -- discriminating controls

At least two RH-false or geometry-broken control families are specified, with a
predicted axiom of failure before the final RH conclusion.

### S4 -- bounded workshop contribution

At least one bounded artifact can be produced here that tests a necessary
compatibility or obstruction and cannot be replaced by checking more zeta
zeros or more finite PSD cells.

## Verdict rules

- `KILL`: any hard kill gate passes.
- `PARK`: no hard kill passes, but the two park conditions pass.
- `SURVIVE, NARROW`: all survival gates pass; the atlas must focus on the
  missing gluing/polarization/closure mechanisms and exclude decorative
  equivalents.
- `SURVIVE, STRONG`: all survival gates pass and a candidate framework already
  proves a new partial positivity theorem with an unconditional prime or zero
  consequence.  This is not expected.

An atlas is built only after a `SURVIVE` verdict.  Survival means the direction
is rational to investigate, not that it is likely to prove RH.
