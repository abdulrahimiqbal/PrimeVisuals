# Verdict — Frobenius prime graph

## PARK AT TWO NAMED OBSTRUCTIONS

The canonical fixed-curve theorem survives as a field-level open problem. The
proposed Atlas mechanism does **not** survive this first round.

### What survives

For

\[
E_0:y^2+y=x^3-x,
\]

the conjectural statement

\[
\pi_{E_0,2}(X)\sim C_{E_0,2}\frac{\sqrt X}{(\log X)^2},
\qquad C_{E_0,2}>0,
\]

is a precise, canonical and consequential fixed-curve problem. Its positive
constant was independently reconstructed, its first published pair was exactly
recovered, and finite congruence data does not determine the global return.
Nothing in this audit kills that conjecture.

### What does not survive

The claimed new object is not new: elliptic amicable pairs are due to
Silverman--Stange, and Jones already defines the finite Frobenius graph and its
positive/zero-constant criterion.

The proposed route—average over curves, concentrate over twists, then amplify
to `E0`—fails both mechanism gates:

1. **Parity:** no stated estimate distinguishes prime group order from
   semiprime order for one fixed curve.
2. **De-averaging:** the frozen 34-twist panel contains one unique cycle event,
   copied across ten twists. Its variance/mean² is `2.4`, not tending toward
   zero, and the required twist-specific local normalization is absent.

Calling this a surviving *mechanism* would therefore violate the frozen
promotion rule.

### Exact reopen condition

Do not reopen because a larger computation finds more cycles. Reopen only with
both of the following written as quantitative lemmas:

1. a prime-sensitive lower-bound or bilinear estimate for
   `#E_d(F_p)` in a squarefree-twist family that cannot be satisfied by the
   corresponding semiprime sequence; and
2. a correctly adelically normalized second-moment estimate with an explicit
   power-saving off-diagonal term, strong enough to imply
   `Var = o(mean^2)` as the twist range and `X` grow.

Until then, the fixed-curve conjecture belongs in the Atlas as an important
known frontier, while the proposed twist-amplifier path is parked rather than
advertised as a breakthrough candidate.
