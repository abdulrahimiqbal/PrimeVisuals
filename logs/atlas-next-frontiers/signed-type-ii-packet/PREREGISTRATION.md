# First survival round — signed Type-II packet dispersion

Frozen: 2026-07-12

## Target

Test the proposed mechanism behind the signed packet theorem before promoting
ABAC: sum the tent packet before Cauchy--Schwarz, cancel the exact local modes,
and seek a factor-H gain for genuine prime-detecting coefficient classes.

## Frozen gates

1. Derive the zero-frequency and finite local-character contribution of the
   tent packet with `Lambda_z#`; cancellation must occur algebraically before
   estimation.
2. On dyadic endpoints `X=2^12,2^13,2^14,2^15`, test the complete signed packet
   for the divisor surrogate `d_2-log`, real `Lambda-1`, and the local model at
   all integer `H` nearest `X^(1/3)`, `X^(5/12)`, and `X^(1/2)/2`.
3. Compare signed-before-norm aggregation against shiftwise absolute values.
   A survival signal is a stable reduction in the fitted H exponent, not a
   small endpoint.
4. Construct a finite balanced Type-II bilinear operator and compare its top
   singular vector with frozen Heath--Brown/Vaughan-style coefficient families,
   random balanced families, and a rational-phase resonator.
5. Kill the proposed coefficient advantage if an allowed prime-detecting family
   saturates the generic packet norm, if zero modes remain of order `XH`, or if
   summing first gives no stable H-exponent gain over controls.

## Scope

Finite tests cannot prove the packet theorem.  They can kill the proposed
spectral/coefficient mechanism.  A numerical survivor remains an analytic
lemma program, not evidence for ABAC.
